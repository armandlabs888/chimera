## 3. Helper Location Check

3.1 **What happens.** When a Helper chooses to verify, we ask their device for its precise location (GPS or similar) one time, with the Helper's permission, and compare it to the home address the Helper provided.

3.2 **What we keep.** We keep the verification result (passed or not passed), the date and time of the check, and the address that was verified. **We do not keep an ongoing record of your movements.** [Confirm with the product: the raw device coordinates are deleted immediately after the comparison, or within [X] days for fraud prevention.]

3.3 **What others see.** Other Users may see that you completed a location check. They do not see your coordinates or your full home address unless you choose to share it.

3.4 **Why.** We use this information only to help confirm that Helpers are local and to deter fake accounts. It is not a background check and does not confirm identity. Precise location is "sensitive personal information" under the CCPA, and we use it only for the purposes the CCPA permits without offering a right to limit, such as verifying accounts, preventing fraud, and providing the feature you asked for.

3.5 **On-site security code.** If we offer an optional on-site security code, we will store the code and whether and when it was confirmed, so the Poster and Helper can confirm the right person arrived.
