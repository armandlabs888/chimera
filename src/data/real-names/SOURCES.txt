Real-name reference data (public-domain / free-to-use)

1. US SSA Baby Names, national files (yobYYYY.txt), all years.
   Names with total count >= 5 across years.
   US government work, public domain.
   Source mirror: Internet Archive item names_202502 (SSA names.zip).
   Original: https://www.ssa.gov/oact/babynames/names.zip

2. International given names supplement.
   smashew/NameDatabases first names/all.txt (free to use).
   https://github.com/smashew/NameDatabases

3. US Census Bureau 2010 Census surnames (Names_2010Census.csv).
   US government work, public domain.
   https://www2.census.gov/topics/genealogy/2010surnames/names.zip

4. Common English words (top ~20k by frequency).
   first20hours/google-10000-english 20k.txt
   https://github.com/first20hours/google-10000-english

Runtime validation imports only lexicon.v1.json (english words plus
english∩given and english∩surname allowlists). Full first-names.v1.json
and surnames.v1.json are bundled for reference and regeneration; they are
not imported by the client bundle.

Regenerate with: node scripts/build-real-name-lexicon.mjs
(Requires the raw source files under /tmp or paths passed via env.)
